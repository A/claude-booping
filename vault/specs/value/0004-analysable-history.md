Plans, runs and their outcomes are kept across every project, so the history can be
analysed: practices taken over from one project to another, benchmarks, quality
comparison, problem analysis.

## In practice

Plans, retros and reviews stay in the vault after they close; their shape stays stable enough to compare across projects and time.

## Trade-off

Storage and a stable artefact shape that is harder to change.
