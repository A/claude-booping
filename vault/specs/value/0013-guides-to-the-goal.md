The system is clear to the user and leads them to the goal by the hand, while letting
a professional step aside and get there their own way.

## In practice

After a chat about a task and a grooming run, the system says how to call develop; given an under-groomed plan, it says so and what to do next.

## Trade-off

Hints the user may not need.
