Booping is a work tool for professionals, so the quality of the result matters. It
spends tokens rationally, without token-maxing, and keeps a balance between
simplicity and the quality of the result.

## In practice

Step prompts are evaluated and narrowed while the evals show the result still holds; a step's prompt is tuned to a specific model and copied and re-tuned for another, with evals working as unit tests for the steps; what a sub-agent already knows when asked about a prompt detail does not belong in the prompt; lessons are written as compact rules with examples, not as a raw retro.

## Trade-off

A run sometimes costs more than the cheapest one that would pass.
