A step gets only what it needs. What the model already knows does not clutter the
context; skills and steps carry only the information that matters — ten lines, not
two hundred, when ten work.

## In practice

Detail is linked and read on demand, never inlined in case it helps; a rule reaches a step as a short brief with a link.

## Trade-off

More files and indirection; a step may miss something it was not handed.
