We know the system works as it should and can guarantee it high
enough to call it reliable. Tests, snapshots, evals and checks are the tools, not the
value.

## In practice

A behaviour that matters is held by a check that would fail if it broke.

## Trade-off

The cost of building and keeping the test infrastructure.
