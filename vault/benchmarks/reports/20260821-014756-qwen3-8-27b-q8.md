## Sprint narrative

Third and final run of the all-at-once experiment: the whole plan in one pi invocation, segment `all-milestones`. The cleanest of the three — one attempt, 19m35s, just 53 tool calls (bash 41, read 8, edit 3, write 1), no retries, no malformed calls, no loops, no deaths, no freezes. The worker delivered the largest corpus of the cohort, 35 cases, splitting the newline/tab gap behaviour into two dedicated cases and adding four orphan cases of extra coverage. Its one visible stumble is a mislabeled case: `append-only-nothing-to-change-exits-1` claims to test an append-only no-op but passes no `--append` at all, duplicating the plain nothing-to-do case. The orchestrator validated with `just ci` only (cross review carries the quality judgement), flipped the bookkeeping and made the per-milestone commits (`de1ec96`, `c1949dc`, `2b75cbf`). Two blind rebaselines in the log — again the whole agentic deduction.

Exact-name recall was the cohort's lowest at 4/34 — this run renamed almost everything — but behaviour mapping recovered 27 more, landing at 31/34 (91.18%), tied with run 1 for the cohort's best. The three unported behaviours: `empty-value-lands-as-an-empty-string` (incidental inside the combined case), `other-keys-comments-and-body-survive-the-set` (no comment-bearing standalone case), and `malformed-append-pair-is-rejected` (no case — missed by all three runs). Gaps 2/3. Four-channel rigour 32/35 (91.43%), wildcard discipline 1 of 559 asserted lines. Four orphan cases add coverage beyond the etalon, one of them redundant (`at-prefixed-value-is-a-literal` repeats an assertion `syntax-sensitive-values-round-trip` already carries).
