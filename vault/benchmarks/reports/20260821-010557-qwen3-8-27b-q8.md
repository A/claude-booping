## The task

The fixed develop-sprint benchmark: port the `frontmatter-update` unit suite into a txtar e2e corpus across three milestones — core CLI cases, list-op and macro cases, then delete the superseded unit file after a coverage cross-check — on a clean clone at baseline `a025189`, with `just ci` green at the end.

## Sprint narrative

Second run of the all-at-once experiment: the whole plan in one pi invocation, segment `all-milestones`. One attempt, 27m04s, 66 tool calls, no retries, no malformed calls, no loops, no deaths, no freezes. The worker delivered a leaner corpus than run 1 — 29 cases against 34 — folding several etalon behaviours into side-assertions of neighbouring cases rather than porting them one-to-one, and wrote its cases through bash heredocs (49 bash calls, a single `write`). It handled the newline/tab gap case cleverly, generating the values through deterministic `printf` macros since a raw newline cannot cross the txtar cmd line. The orchestrator validated with `just ci` only (cross review carries the quality judgement this run), flipped the bookkeeping, and made the per-milestone commits (`e4127b9`, `057d371`, `3770600`). The log shows 2 blind rebaselines — the whole agentic deduction.

## Gates

All six pass: ci, determinism, e2e, milestones (3/3 terminal), scope, unit_deleted. Nothing was close.

## Corpus quality

Exact-name recall 8/34; the case-mapping judge lifted it to 29/34 (85.29%) — the lowest mapped recall of the three all-at-once runs' cohort so far. Five behaviours unported as dedicated cases: `malformed-append-pair-is-rejected` and `value-may-contain-equals-signs` have no case at all, while `empty-value-lands-as-an-empty-string`, `summary-goes-to-stderr-not-stdout` and `other-keys-comments-and-body-survive-the-set` live only as side-assertions inside other cases. Gaps 2/3 — the malformed-append gap missed again. Four-channel rigour is the run's strength: 28/29 (96.55%), and wildcard discipline tight at 1 of 545 asserted lines. No orphan cases — every branch case maps.
