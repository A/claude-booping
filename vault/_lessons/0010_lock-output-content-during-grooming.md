---
title: Lock the exact content of any user-facing output or log format with the user during grooming
targets:
  - groom/draft-plan
---

When a plan defines a user-facing output or log format, lock its exact *content* with the user during design alignment (line fields, truncation rules, trailers) — not just the format in prose. Settling content mid-develop iterates the output contract live instead of deciding it up front.
