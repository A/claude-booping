# ADR scorer v3: routing, overlaps and missing cheap options from the specs guide and the record inventory

И пройдись по этим проектам, погоняй там вот эти эксперименты, вот, и заполируй промпт этого скорера, получается.

## Findings and decisions (2026-10-19, owner: "заполируй промпт этого скорера"; "С, очевидно, надо брать")

Experiment material (prompts, runner, raw results — they include other projects' records): vault/notes/references/20261019-adr-scorer-experiment/ (private note, not published). Aggregates:

- Arms on 12 labelled ADRs (6 the owner deleted on 2026-10-18 + 6 kept): text-only rubric (today's scorer) cannot route; + specs guide routes 2.5/6; + guide + the tier's record inventory (title: summary of value/rule/adr) + the project's primitives routes 4.5/6 and catches 6/6; Sonnet on the same input misses 3/6; Opus with repo read access costs ×6 and routes 1/6. Chosen: guide + inventory, Opus, no tools.
- Mutants (a cheap option cut from a good ADR): the cut option is named in `missing` 4/4; the score drops 1/4 — the list, not the score, is the signal.
- Gate: block when route ≠ adr or alt_score ≤ 1; otherwise pass and put `missing` into the brief as "Не взвешено:" and `overlaps` into "Задевает:". On 70+ ADRs across three projects it blocked the owner-deleted ones and the audit's "shaky" ones and passed the stack choices.
- Prompt v3 (task-v3.md in the material) answers one JSON line: route, route_why, overlaps (≤3 bare refs), alt_score, missing (≤3, naming the existing primitive or record).
- The inventory must hold only records that exist when the ADR is written (the experiment had to filter by created_at).
- Cost per ADR ≈ 7K tokens in, 650 out on Opus.
- The scorer agent keeps `tools: ListAgents` (allowlist); its body must stop saying it has no tools.
- Eval fixtures stay booping-only (public repo): deleted ADRs from git history (0012 → rule, 0013 → none, 0005 → value), mutants of 0003/0007/0030/0033.
