# Specs written through a subagent vs the driver owning vault writes

## Question

Who writes the records: the specs subagent, or the driver from its drafts?

## Context

adr/0034 §4: every read or write of the specs goes through a subagent. The project tier is the vault root, and rule/0019 says the driver does every vault read and write. adr/0012 is gone; rule/0019 now carries the split.

## Proposal

The subagent drafts and the driver writes; reword adr/0034 §4 to say so. Alternative: an explicit carve-out in rule/0019 for the specs subagent.
