## session-stats (owner, 2026-10-18)

Kept as a CLI primitive. It knows nothing of plans: it mines the `sessions:` list of any artifact (plan, review, retro, a user's own) into `metrics_*` frontmatter. Today only develop's `stamp-active-time` hook calls it; the readers are groom's `latest_plans` columns and the `sprints.md` base. It passes rule/0032.
