# Opus-only judgement vs prompts re-tuned per model

## Question

Should a cheaper model ever be benchmarked for judgement steps?

## Context

rule/0031 says judgement runs on Opus, a cheaper model is not benchmarked for judgement work, and "the playbooks are measured on Opus". value/0012's In practice says a step's prompt is tuned to a model and copied and re-tuned for another. adr/0022's evidence came from Qwen 27B. On 2026-10-18 the researcher moved to Sonnet high.

## Proposal

Narrow value/0012's In practice to "tuned on the model the step runs on", and allow benchmarking a cheaper model in rule/0031 while keeping shipping on Opus. Drop the "measured on Opus" claim or scope it to groom and develop.
