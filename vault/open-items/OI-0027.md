# Story points: groom deflates its own estimates

## Question

Which of (a)-(e) go into the first plan?

## Context

Source: [lightspace-android feedback](../researches/20261018-lightspace-android-feedback/index.md), points 15-17, data in [20261018-sp-experiment](../researches/20261018-lightspace-android-feedback/sp-experiment/).

In booping's own vault, after the ≥5 SP gate (adr/0022), 97% of tasks are 1-2 SP, plans pile up at 34-35 SP, and 11 milestones have `sp` ≠ the sum of their tasks. Ten blind Opus estimators scored the same 44 tasks about 2.4× higher than groom under every prompt condition, so the cause is the plan author scoring its own tasks while fitting thresholds. Owner's semantics: a task at 5 SP is research; a milestone sums to ≤15; a worker group ≤15; a sprint ≤35. Related: OI-0010 (adr/0022 packing).

## Proposal

Owner, 2026-10-18: do it now. Proposed package: (a) a blind estimation step, a fresh agent seeing only the tasks and the scale; (b) anchor examples per scale value; (c) `plan check` flags milestone sp ≠ task sum and plans with >60% 1-SP tasks; (d) start/end recorded per milestone so retro compares SP to minutes; (e) SP/h per worker.

## Recheck (2026-10-18)

Computed over the 224 milestone files with a Tasks table (527 tasks; older plans with inline milestones not counted):

- Task SP: 1 = 58%, 2 = 34%, 3 = 6%, 4 = 2%, 5 = 0. Before 2026-08-21: 74 / 21 / 4 / 2%; since: 51 / 40 / 7 / 1%. 3 and 4 are rare, not absent.
- Milestone SP: 3 = 91, 4 = 66, 2 = 37, ≥5 = 28 of 224 — 70% sit at 3-4.
- Plan SP 28-36: 33 → 0 plans, 34 → 5, 35 → 5; the squeeze under 35 holds.
- 11 milestones have `sp` ≠ the sum of their tasks; the count holds.
- The gate is on the milestone, not the task: `playbooks/groom/_schemas/milestone.yaml` holds milestone `sp` to `^[1-4]$` (`core.sprint.redecompose_threshold: 5`), and adr/0022 says "a milestone with sp >= redecompose_threshold" is a hard groom gate. The owner's semantics put 5 on a task (research) and cap a milestone at 15. A milestone capped at 4 leaves its 2-3 tasks at 1-2 SP each, so the cap alone explains most of the 1-2 pile, without assuming the author deflates.
- The blind experiment has no ground truth: blind scorers 2.4× higher shows the two disagree, not which one is right. Ground truth is available: develop makes one commit per milestone, so commit-to-commit time gives a duration per milestone to correlate each estimate against.
