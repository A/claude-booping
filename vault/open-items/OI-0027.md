# Story points: groom deflates its own estimates

## Question

## Context

Source: [lightspace-android feedback](../notes/20261018-lightspace-android-feedback.md), points 15-17, data in [20261018-sp-experiment](../notes/20261018-sp-experiment/).

In booping's own vault, after the ≥5 SP gate (adr/0022), 97% of tasks are 1-2 SP, plans pile up at 34-35 SP, and 11 milestones have `sp` ≠ the sum of their tasks. Ten blind Opus estimators scored the same 44 tasks about 2.4× higher than groom under every prompt condition, so the cause is the plan author scoring its own tasks while fitting thresholds. Owner's semantics: a task at 5 SP is research; a milestone sums to ≤15; a worker group ≤15; a sprint ≤35. Related: OI-0010 (adr/0022 packing).

## Proposal

Owner, 2026-10-18: do it now. Proposed package: (a) a blind estimation step, a fresh agent seeing only the tasks and the scale; (b) anchor examples per scale value; (c) `plan check` flags milestone sp ≠ task sum and plans with >60% 1-SP tasks; (d) start/end recorded per milestone so retro compares SP to minutes; (e) SP/h per worker.
