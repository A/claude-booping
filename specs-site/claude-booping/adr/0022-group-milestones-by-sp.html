    Develop groups milestones per worker by story points, not by count

    acceptednot accepted

    Develop packs milestones per worker greedily within one wave under a max_sp cap of 15 instead of max_milestones_per_agent, and sp ≥ 5 is a hard groom gate.

    Context§

    Develop briefs one worker per group of milestones, capped by core.sprint.max_milestones_per_agent: 2. A worker's real limit is its session context; milestone count does not track it, story points roughly do and are the only size signal already in the plan. Every clean comparison in booping-benchmarks (one fixed 3-milestone, 8-SP refactoring plan, local Qwen3.8 27B, 2026-08..09) has one delegation for the whole plan match or beat one delegation per milestone, in less time, with fewer output tokens and a tighter spread:

    Date Model Whole plan (n) Per milestone (n) Output tokens Report
    08-21 27B Q8 95.9 (3), spread 0.4 ≈95.6 (8), spread 6.5 36–47k vs 55–80k reports/20260821-014756-qwen3-8-27b-q8.md
    08-23 / 09-03 27B FP8 97.0 (3) 91.9 (3) 54–96k vs 74–76k reports/20260823-002017-…, reports/20260903-044651-…
    09-13 27B, new template 96.3 (1) 91.1 (3) 58k vs 75–88k reports/20260913-114521-qwen3-8-27b.md
    09-16..17 27B 93.8–97.2 (6) 89.9–92.5 (09-12/13) — reports/20260916-232343-qwen3-8-27b.md

    The 09-03 against 09-12 pair changed the chat template together with the delegation shape; the 09-13 control isolates the delegation and keeps the 5-point gap. Ten historical plans had a median of about 23 SP over 5–7 milestones; 13 of 59 milestones had sp ≥ 5, and groom ignored the redecompose threshold in 5 of 10 plans.

    Decision§

    core.sprint.max_sp: 15 replaces max_milestones_per_agent. Milestones pack greedily and contiguously in graph order while the group's sum(sp) <= max_sp, only within one topological wave; a single milestone is always a valid group and is never split. Grouping changes only the briefing; the runner still validates, commits and transitions per milestone. Groom gets a hard gate: a milestone with sp >= redecompose_threshold (5) does not leave grooming.

    The cap measures review burden, not how hard the work is for the agent: it bounds how much one briefing hands the human to check at once (owner, 2026-10-16), so it is tuned to the reviewer, not to the model.

    SP cap of 15§

    chosencomplexity: lowreversibility: high

    Halves delegations on the median plan

    One knob; greedy packing within a wave.

    • Pros: simulation roughly halves the delegations on the median plan; a warm context avoids re-briefing and repeated repo reads.
    • Cons: quality is measured only at 8 SP per delegation (n=3 against n=3, one plan, one task type); 15 is a ×2 extrapolation; SP measures human effort, not tokens.
    • Complexity: low.
    • Reversibility: high — the knob is cheap to lower.

    Count cap of two§

    rejectedcomplexity: lowreversibility: high

    Cuts well below the measured floor

    The current rule: up to two milestones per briefing.

    • Pros: not named in the source.
    • Cons: unrelated to context use; more delegations than needed.
    • Complexity: low.
    • Reversibility: high.

    A 10 to 15 band with rounding§

    rejectedcomplexity: lowreversibility: high

    Same group counts as the plain cap

    A cap range rather than one number.

    • Pros: not named in the source.
    • Cons: over the historical plans it gave the same group counts as plain max_sp: 15, so the simpler knob won.
    • Complexity: low.
    • Reversibility: high.

    Consequences§

    • core.sprint.max_sp (default 15) replaced max_milestones_per_agent, and groom keeps a milestone at or over redecompose_threshold from leaving drafting (BL-0015).
    • Planned calibration: re-groom a 31-SP plan with sp-8 milestones and run it as a second benchmark in two groups of about 15.
    • 2026-10-18 (owner): redecompose_threshold (5 SP) is a threshold on one task — a task at it is research and must be split — and never was one on a milestone. A milestone is the sum of its tasks, grouping an isolated large piece of work, capped only by the group budget max_sp. The milestone-level gate this record introduced is a mistake; groom gates tasks at 5 instead, and playbooks/groom/_schemas/milestone.yaml stops holding milestone sp to 1-4. Measured effect of the old gate: 70% of milestones at 3-4 SP and 92% of tasks at 1-2 SP (OI-0027).
