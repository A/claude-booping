    A faster groom without losing quality

    acceptednot accepted

    Try several groom playbook variants and keep the one that runs faster with no loss in plan quality.

    Idea§

    The owner wants to try different variants of the groom playbook, with one goal: find a groom that runs faster without losing quality (owner, 2026-10-15). At the time a plan of 12-29 SP spent 6-12 minutes in groom on top of 17-43 minutes of develop, and small plans paid the same fixed groom cost as large ones. Variants to compare included skipping groom for small mechanical plans (straight to develop with a short brief), a lighter graph, and depth scaled to the task, which went on as idea/0009.

    A measurement (reference/0006) puts the playbook groom at 2.4× the wall time, 5.2× the main-thread API calls and 3.0× the output tokens of the old /groom skill. The measurement does not say which steps the extra cost came from.

    How to check§

    • Experiment (owner, 2026-10-16; protocol in booping-benchmarks groom/): a corpus of past requests from two other projects (2 small, 2 medium, 2 complex), each in an isolated worktree at the commit before its plan with a vault stripped of plans, retros, notes and reviews; variants V0 the current groom, V1 a bare "write a plan", V2 bare + plan template, V3 template + stages, V4 V3 + cross-review (codex, fable, three OpenRouter models via pi-developer); metrics wall time, tokens and calls from booping session-stats, plan quality by a blind rubric judge against the historical plan, and for the best two a develop run scored by bench-score; each groom prompt paragraph probed against an empty-context agent to cut what the model already knows. Results land in a reference record, the chosen shape in an ADR.
    • Added 2026-10-16 (owner): D1 — the whole development as one claude -p run with no groom or develop playbooks, compared on time and outcome with the historical pipeline; every variant runs in two isolation modes — a clean vault, and a vault with the project's plans, retros and reviews dated before the task (realistic: past plans may guide the model); historical reference plans came from older booping versions, so fresh references are made with the current booping at the end and the judge checks against both. Corpus: 6 done tasks, mostly web/client work (booping-benchmarks groom/corpus/).
    • Owner's targets: small tasks (10-15 SP) reach develop with little or no grooming; complex ones keep research quality and also get faster; tokens about the same or lower.
    • Run each variant on the same set of requests on the same commit, and compare plan quality (the groom evals' rubric, the plan's later develop outcome: rework, failed attempts) and cost (booping session-stats: wall time, tokens, API calls); a variant wins when its cost is clearly lower and quality holds. booping-benchmarks holds the harness for such runs.

    Verdict§

    2026-10-17: the benchmark (booping-benchmarks groom/, reference/0007) picked one drafting pass on the plan template, research then draft, with no self-review step (adr/0030): about 8 minutes a plan against 13–23 for the old groom, at equal or better judged quality. A script cuts the drafted plan into milestone files (adr/0031), and groom runs no separate review step (adr/0035). The light path for small, already-decided requests and depth scaled to the task are not done yet; they went on as idea/0009.
