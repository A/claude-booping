Booping decomposes work into plans and their sprints, so a project can be written along its roadmap, sprint by sprint; once work runs in sprints, a manager that carries it through grooming, development, review and merge follows naturally (owner, 2026-10-18).

## In practice

The project-manager playbook files every request as an item and works the items through groom, develop and code-review in the project's autonomy mode.

## Trade-off

Even a small request passes through the item store before it is worked.
