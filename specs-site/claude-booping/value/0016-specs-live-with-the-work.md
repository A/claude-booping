Specs are updated in the course of the work, not in a separate pass the user has to remember, and they
do not go stale. They are applied wherever they add quality to a result and knowledge of the decisions
already made (owner, 2026-10-16).

## In practice

A decision made in a groom, a scenario the owner describes in passing, a departure a sprint makes
from an accepted ADR — each reaches the specs while the work happens, as a draft the owner confirms.
A playbook that decides looks at what the specs already settle instead of deciding again.

## Trade-off

Some cost on every run that touches the specs, and drafts the owner has to confirm.
